Title: Vacation Planner App

The purpose of this application is to allow users to easily manage vacation plans by inputting a vacation title, the name of the hotel, and the start and end dates of the vacation. Users can also add planned excursions and their associated dates to their book. This information can be added, edited, deleted, and shared at will.

**INSTRUCTIONS:**

(B1) Adding a vacation:
1. Tap the "Start" button.
2. Tap the add/+ button in the bottom right corner of the "Vacation List" screen.
3. Fill out title, hotel, start and end date fields.
4. Open the more options menu in the top right corner of the screen.
5. Tap "Save Vacation."
<br>(B3) NOTE: Ensure the start and end dates are formatted properly (mm/dd/yyyy) and the start date is before the end date to avoid an error when saving

<br><br>
(B1) Editing a vacation:
1. Tap the title of the vacation from the "Vacation List" screen.
2. Modify the information as desired.
3. Open the more options menu in the top right corner of the screen.
4. Tap "Save Vacation."

<br><br>
(B1) Deleting a vacation:
1. Tap the title of the vacation from the "Vacation List" screen.
2. Open the more options menu in the top right corner of the screen.
3. Tap "Delete Vacation."

<br><br>
(B3) Display vacation details:
1. Tap the title of the vacation from the "Vacation List" screen.
2. Vacation details are displayed with associated excursions in the list below.

<br><br>
(B3) Set notifications for a vacation:
1. Tap the title of the vacation from the "Vacation List" screen.
2. Open the more options menu in the top right corner of the screen.
3. Tap "Notify me on start date" for a notification on the start date of the vacation.
4. Tap "Notify me on end date" for a notification on the end date of the vacation.

<br><br>
(B3) Share vacation details:
1. Tap the title of the vacation from the "Vacation List" screen.
2. Open the more options menu in the top right corner of the screen.
3. Tap "Share Vacation."
4. Select your preferred sharing option (clipboard, email, SMS, etc.)

<br><br>
(B5) Create an excursion:
1. Tap any vacation title from the "Vacation List" screen.
2. Tap the add/+ button in the bottom right corner of the vacation details screen.
3. Fill out the fields for excursion title and date.
4. Select the correct vacation for the excursion from the drop-down menu.
5. Open the more options menu in the top right corner of the screen.
6. Tap "Save Excursion."
<br>NOTE: Excursions will not save if the date is not within the date range of the associated vacation

<br><br>
(B5) Edit an excursion:
1. Tap the title of the vacation associated with your target excursion from the "Vacation List" screen.
2. Tap the title of your target excursion, located below the vacation details.
3. Modify the information as desired.
4. Open the more options menu in the top right corner of the screen.
5. Tap "Save Excursion."

<br><br>
(B5) Delete an excursion:
1. Tap the title of the vacation associated with your target excursion from the "Vacation List" screen.
2. Tap the title of your target excursion, located below the vacation details.
3. Open the more options menu in the top right corner of the screen.
4. Tap "Delete Excursion."

<br><br>
(B5) View excursion details:
1. Tap the title of the vacation associated with your target excursion from the "Vacation List" screen.
2. Tap the title of your target excursion, located below the vacation details.
3. Excursion details are displayed.

<br><br>
(B5) Set notification for excursion date
1. Tap the title of the vacation associated with your target excursion from the "Vacation List" screen.
2. Tap the title of your target excursion, located below the vacation details.
3. Open the more options menu in the top right corner of the screen.
4. Tap "Notify Me."
